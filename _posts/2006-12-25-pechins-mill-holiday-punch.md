Serves 8

## Ingredients

- 2 quarts eggnog
- 1 quart vanilla ice cream
- 1 cup rum
- 1/3 cup brandy
- 4 black cats plus 1 gray tabby cat
- 1 beige Shepherd-Husky dog
- Ground cinnamon and nutmeg to taste

Pour eggnog, rum, and brandy into an extra-large punch bowl. Spoon in ice cream, dog, cats and spices. Stir to combine and watch the holiday chaos ensue.
