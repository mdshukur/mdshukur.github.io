## Itinerary

### Sunday

- 2:00 PM — Left Rhinebeck, NY
- 5:30 PM — Departed Newark Airport  
  (parked at FastTrack, may try ABC Airport Parking next time)

### Monday

- 7:30 AM — Arrived at Arlanda Airport, Stockholm
- 8:00 AM — Retrieved bags and took taxi
- 8:30 AM — Arrived at Christina’s spacious penthouse co-op
- 10:00 AM — Walked to Gamla Stan
  - Saw palace exterior
  - Watched changing of the guard
  - Walked along the water
  - Lunch at Cafe Nova
  - Bought postcards
  - Visited Stockholm Cathedral  
    (highlight: St. George and the Dragon statue)

![Stockholm](/assets/img/uploads/2013/10/196966123-S.jpg)

- Returned to apartment
- Evening walk near Stadshuset (City Hall)
- Dinner with Christina
- Chocolate, wine, and photos late at night

![Evening in Sweden](/assets/img/uploads/2013/10/199983203-S.jpg)

### Tuesday

- Subway to Central Terminal
- Bus to Nynäshamn
- Ferry to Visby, Gotland
- Checked into Hotel Villa Borgen
- Explored church ruins around town
- Dinner at upscale pizza restaurant

### Wednesday

- Continental breakfast at hotel
- Explored more church ruins and botanical gardens
- Grocery store lunch supplies
- Rented used Opel manual-transmission car
- Visited Jungfrun at Lickershamn
- Ferry to Fårö
- Saw Ingmar Bergman’s grave
- Viewed Raukar limestone formations
- Dinner at Crêperie & Logi in Visby

### Thursday

- Early ferry back to Stockholm
- Visited the Vasa Museum

![Vasa Museum](/assets/img/uploads/2013/10/199982934-S.jpg)

- Coffee break at café
- Market visit
- Italian dinner
- Watched episodes of *Freaks and Geeks*

### Friday

- Garden and organic lunch
- Nordic Museum
- Shopping at N&K
- Gamla Stan
- Watched *Death at a Funeral*

### Saturday

- Lunch outside Nobel Museum
- Royal Armory visit
- Stockholm City Hall (outside only)
- Nobel Museum
- Checked into Radisson Royal Viking
- Dinner at Christina’s

### Sunday

- Took airport express train
- Flight to Newark
- Left parking lot around 12:30 PM

## Costs

- Airfare: $700 per person (Newark → Stockholm direct)
- Gotland ferry: 250 SEK (~$40)
- Subway pass: 160 SEK for 8 trips
- Taxi airport → Stockholm: 395 SEK (~$70)
- Hotel Villa Borgen: 990 SEK/night (~$150)
- Italian dinner: 900 SEK (~$140)
- Ferry lunch: 170 SEK (~$16)
- McDonald’s meal: 55 SEK (~$7.50)
